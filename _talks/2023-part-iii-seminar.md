Hegselmann-Krause framework, covering the conditions under which a population of
bounded-confidence agents converges to consensus, polarization, or fragmentation.

Related project: [Predicting Opinion Phases in the Hegselmann-Krause Model](/projects/opinion-dynamics-HK/).
